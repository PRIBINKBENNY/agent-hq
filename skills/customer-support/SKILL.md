# Customer support for a small AI product business

- Triage every message into: bug, how-do-I, billing, feature request, complaint, other. Urgent = the product is down
  for a paying customer or data is at risk.
- Service levels (say them, keep them): first reply within 4 working hours, bug acknowledged with a ticket id, fix or
  workaround within 2 working days, weekly update on anything open longer.
- Reply shape: thank, restate the problem in one line so they know you understood, what you did or will do, when
  they will hear next, one question if you need information. Under 120 words. No jargon.
- Reproduce before answering a bug: steps, expected, actual, version, logs. If you cannot reproduce, ask for one
  specific thing (a screenshot, the exact time), not "more details".
- Escalate to Pribin: anything about money, legal wording, a promise of a new feature, a complaint about an agent's
  behaviour, or a customer threatening to leave. Draft the reply, do not send it.
- Keep a knowledge base: every answered how-do-I becomes a short article in workspace/support/kb/ with a title the
  customer would search for; link it in the next reply instead of retyping.
- Measure: open tickets, median first-reply time, reopened tickets, the top three causes this month; put them in the
  Monday board pack.
- Never argue, never blame the customer, never say "as I said". Apologise once, fix, confirm.
