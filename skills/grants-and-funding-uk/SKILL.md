# Grants, competitions and free programmes for a UK AI and robotics founder

- Innovate UK runs the main grant competitions (Innovation Funding Service). In 2026 the robotics money went to
  adoption hubs, a convening body and skills programmes, mostly for consortia with grant requests of £100k-£2m;
  individual founders join as partners of a university or SME rather than apply alone.
- Innovation Loans: non-dilutive but repayable, for close-to-market projects by registered SMEs; not for ideas.
- Realistic entry points for one person: Innovate UK Business Connect events and partner-finding; university
  spin-out and incubator programmes (free desk, mentoring, sometimes small grants); robotics competitions with
  prizes; hackathons run by labs and manufacturers; open-source grants from foundations.
- Before applying: a registered company (free to set up in a day), a one-page proposal with the problem, the user,
  the evidence, the team and the ask; a demo video; a budget in a spreadsheet. Assessors score what is written,
  not what is meant.
- Grant writing rules: answer the question asked, in the order asked; quantify the market with a cited number; name
  the risks and how they are retired; match the call's wording; never promise what the budget cannot deliver.
- Track every opportunity in a table: name, closing date, eligibility, amount, fit score, next action. Closed
  competitions reopen in later rounds; note the pattern.
- Not advice: eligibility and dates change; confirm on the official service before any application.
