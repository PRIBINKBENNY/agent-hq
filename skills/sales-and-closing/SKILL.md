# Sales and closing for freelance AI work

- Discovery before proposal: what breaks today, how often, what it costs them, who decides, when they need it. Five
  questions, written answers in the brain. A proposal without discovery is a guess.
- Proposal shape: their problem in their words; what they get (deliverables, dates, what is excluded); price and
  terms; proof (demo link, case study, a screenshot); next step with a date. One page. Send within 24 hours of the
  conversation.
- Pricing: fixed price for defined scope (the £450 starter); day rate only for undefined work; 50% deposit on
  acceptance for anything above £300; invoice on delivery.
- Objections: "too expensive" -> compare with the cost of the problem; "not now" -> agree a date and put it in the
  pipeline; "we have a tool" -> ask what it does not do; "need to think" -> ask what would make it a yes.
- Follow-up cadence: day 3 (a useful extra, one question), day 7 (short check-in), day 14 (last message, leave the
  door open). Then stop; mark lost with the reason.
- Record every stage in the deals panel (sent, won, lost, paid); the office reads revenue from it.
- Never: discounts without removing scope, promises on behalf of Pribin, claims the demo cannot show, pressure.

proposal and deal records.
