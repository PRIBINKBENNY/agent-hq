# LinkedIn in 2026 (updated 2026-10-18)
- Write for depth: saves, dwell time and substantive comments count more than likes. End with a question people can
  answer from experience.
- Formats: document carousels (PDF) tend to perform best; structured "how I built X / the 5 steps" posts beat opinions.
  Native short video works; polls are contested.
- Length: roughly 800-1,000 characters for most posts; long-form for occasional deep dives.
- Niche consistency: the feed matches post topics to the profile. Keep posts on Pribin's lanes (ML/CV, agents, building).
- External links reduce reach (size disputed): put the link in the first comment unless it is the point of the post.
- Cadence: 2-4 quality posts a week beats daily filler. Engagement pods and hashtag stacks do not help.
- Generic AI-sounding text is what gets ignored: use specifics, numbers, screenshots of real work.
