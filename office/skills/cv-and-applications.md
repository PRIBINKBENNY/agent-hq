# CVs, cover letters and screening in the UK (updated 2026-10-18)
- ATS mostly RANK applications; few recruiters auto-reject on content. Keyword match still matters: mirror the posting's
  exact skill words where they are true, in a plain single-column layout (no tables, text boxes or images for content).
- AI-written text is easy to spot and screens increasingly look for it: write specific, first-hand sentences (numbers,
  tools, outcomes). Never generic lists like "communication, teamwork, leadership".
- Cover letter: 250-350 words, name the role and company in the first lines, one concrete detail from the posting,
  one proof point with a result. Many UK recruiters still value a tailored letter.
- Consistency: recruiters cross-check the CV against LinkedIn. Titles, dates and claims must match his profile exactly.
- Portfolio proof beats adjectives: link one or two project write-ups or repos with measured results.
- Truth rule (non-negotiable): every claim must exist in the brain's profile or master CV; cv_truth_check before approval.
- UK specifics: no photo, no date of birth; right to work stated plainly (Graduate visa, no sponsorship needed until Oct 2027).
