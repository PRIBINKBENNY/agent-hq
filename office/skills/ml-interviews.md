# ML / AI engineer interviews (updated 2026-10-18)
- Typical loop: recruiter screen, 1-2 coding or ML-fundamentals rounds (live, shared editor, 45-60 min), ML/LLM system
  design, a take-home or case study, behavioural. Usually 4-6 stages over 3-6 weeks.
- LLM system design is now common: design a RAG chatbot, a multi-agent system, an eval plan, a guardrailed tool-using
  agent. Classical ML design still appears but is a minority of the time.
- Drill what is asked: retrieval choices (chunking, hybrid search, re-ranking), evaluation (offline sets, LLM-as-judge
  limits, online metrics), latency/cost trade-offs, failure modes (hallucination, prompt injection), monitoring.
- Take-homes are defended live: practise explaining decisions, trade-offs and what you would do with more time.
- AI-tool use during interviews varies by company: tell Pribin to ask the recruiter; never assume it is allowed.
- Behavioural: STAR stories with numbers, from his real projects (the agent office, Gait Guardian, YOLOv8 work).
Sources: f1jobs.io (ML engineer interview loop 2026), careery.pro (AI engineer interview questions 2026), Glassdoor
reviews (Scale London 2026, ML6). Single-company reviews are anecdotal.
