# Short-form video: Shorts, Reels, TikTok (updated 2026-10-18)
- Hook in the first 1.5-3 seconds with a direct promise AND on-screen text (many watch without sound). Caption everything.
- Length by payoff: 15-30 s for one visual payoff, 30-60 s for one clear answer, 60-90 s for a demo. Reels over 3 minutes
  are not recommended to new audiences.
- Retention and shares matter most; check the retention graph and cut where viewers drop.
- Film once in vertical 9:16, export per platform; post every 1-3 days when possible.
- For Pribin: show real screens (the agent office floor, a model running, a before/after), one idea per video.
Sources: joinbrands.com (YouTube Shorts best practices 2026), reap.video (video length 2026), postigniter.com (hooks),
EICTA IIT Kanpur (short video strategy 2026), Instagram guidance on Reels length. Treat numbers as starting points.
